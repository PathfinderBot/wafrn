Advanced Search Help

You can add advanced filters by prefixing filter keywords in the following list with plus sign + as an additive filter or minus sign - as a subtractive filter. Select filters are separated from their value by either a colon : or equal sign =, for example +target:EvilUser. Select filters can be used implicitly as additive filters without a plus sign +.

Additive filters are OR within their category but AND with different keywords. Subtractive filters are AND with themselves and other keywords.

Here are the existing keywords:

  • r or reporter (Select) URL of the user who reported the target
  • t or target (Select) URL of the user who was reported
  • d or resolved (Flag) whether a report was resolved

Examples:

  • +resolved filters to only resolved reports
  • -r=ReportSpammer -d filters to unresolved reports not sent by ReportSpammer
  • +reporter=Vigilante r:VigilanteAlt -target=EvilUser filters to only reports of user EvilUser that were sent by Vigilante or VigilanteAlt
